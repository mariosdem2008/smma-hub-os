# 15 Explicit Non Goals

## 1. Product Non-Goals

SMMAHUB is not trying to become:

- a general-purpose CRM
- a generic PSA or finance suite
- a blank AI agent builder
- a broad enterprise content platform
- a full social media publishing platform
- a replacement for human strategists or account leads
- a content tool with agency branding
- a dashboard with AI buttons

## 2. Delivery Non-Goals

- Do not optimize for feature count over coherence.
- Do not ship autonomy without evidence, grading, and review.
- Do not mirror agency chaos as a product feature.
- Do not let client-facing polish hide weak internal workflows.
- Do not add workflows whose only purpose is helping someone start an agency from scratch.

## 3. Commercial Non-Goals

- Do not compete on low-end commodity AI pricing.
- Do not sell the product as a cheaper clone of all-in-one tools.
- Do not promise "full automation" as the primary value story.
- Do not build, price, or market for Tier 1 aspirational agencies, solo freelancers, or beginners.
- Do not offer a free plan or any tier below the EUR199/month floor.
