# Subject Inventory

## 1. Purpose

This appendix lists the stable subject domains SMMAHUB should support through governed subject packs.

## 2. Core Domains

| Domain | Example pack types | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Agency model | agency, offer | Encodes how the agency sells and delivers |
| Client brand | client, compliance | Grounds work in client truth and boundaries |
| Strategy | workflow, offer | Shapes diagnoses, recommendations, and briefs |
| Channel execution | channel, workflow | Constrains platform-specific output behavior |
| Collaboration | workflow, compliance | Governs approvals, summaries, and client-visible communication |
| Reporting | workflow, compliance | Shapes performance summaries and next actions |

## 3. Inventory Rules

- create packs for stable business domains, not one-off prompts
- avoid duplicate pack scopes
- assign one accountable owner per pack
- review pack usage and retirement regularly
