# 19 Monetization And Unit Economics

## 1. Pricing Principle

SMMAHUB prices on operational value, trust, and leverage for Tier 2 operating agencies. The floor is EUR199/month; nothing is sold below that floor and there is no no-cost plan.

## 2. Commercial Model

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| Base platform fee | Agency workspace, governance, core workflows |
| Usage tier | Seats, clients, or governed AI volume |
| Premium add-ons | Advanced portal, specialist agents, deeper integrations |
| Services | Onboarding, migration, operating-model setup |

## 3. Locked Tier Frame

| Tier | Monthly price | Fit |
|---|---:|---|
| Operate | EUR199/mo | Established agency, up to roughly 10 active clients |
| Scale | EUR349/mo | Growing agency, up to roughly 25 active clients, multi-seat team |
| Agency | EUR499/mo | Larger team, advanced governance, higher client volume |
| Custom | Custom | Larger agencies or non-standard governance and migration needs |

There is no no-cost tier, no non-ICP tier, and no self-serve playground. A time-boxed trial, if offered, is gated by qualification or card and framed as serious evaluation.

## 4. Unit Economics Logic

- Gross margin depends on disciplined AI orchestration and bounded retrieval.
- Retention depends on daily workflow relevance, not occasional generation usage.
- Expansion should come from more clients, more teams, and higher-governance workflows.
- ACV modeling should use EUR2.4k-EUR6k+ per year, based on the EUR199-EUR499/month range, not low-end content-tool ARPU.
- Market sizing should use the Tier 2 segment of roughly 50k-150k operating agencies globally.

## 5. Pricing Guardrails

- Do not anchor the product as a cheap AI content tool.
- Do not present a no-cost plan or any below-floor plan as current or target pricing.
- Do not name an entry tier in a way that implies low-end enablement.
- Keep usage pricing understandable.
- Protect margins by gating high-cost workflows with value and approval.
- Ensure premium tiers reflect real workflow leverage, not feature clutter.
