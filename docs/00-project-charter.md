## 1. Mission

Build the operating system that lets Tier 2 social media marketing agencies encode their expertise once and apply it across every client through governed AI, structured workflows, and premium collaboration.

## 2. Problem Statement

Tier 2 agencies with 5-25 active clients and 2-10 person teams operate through fragmented tools, duplicated context, inconsistent quality control, and founder bottlenecks. Generic AI tools accelerate output volume, but they do not preserve strategy, approvals, evidence, or client trust.

## 3. Product Thesis

SMMAHUB should unify five things that agencies usually manage separately:

1. Agency operating rules.
2. Client operating records.
3. Strategy generation.
4. Execution and approval workflows.
5. Client-facing collaboration.

## 4. Strategic Promise

**Turn an agency's expertise into a repeatable operating system.**

That means:

- serve established operators, not non-ICP operators
- define the agency model once
- reuse it across every client
- keep strategy connected to execution
- keep client-facing work reviewable and traceable
- let AI operate under explicit rules instead of prompt improvisation

## 5. Success Criteria

- An agency can onboard a client without rebuilding its process.
- Strategy generation improves because context quality improves.
- Work moves through approvals with less chaos and less rewrite work.
- Clients experience a more professional and transparent service model.
- AI reduces supervision overhead instead of increasing it.

## 6. Product Boundaries

SMMAHUB will not:

- replace human strategists
- serve excluded low-end agency segments
- function as a low-end generation app, AI-button dashboard, or no-cost acquisition funnel
- become a generic CRM
- become a full PSA or finance suite
- become a broad social publishing suite
- become a horizontal agent marketplace

## 7. Primary Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Agency owner | Defines operating model and commercial value for a Tier 2 agency |
| Strategist | Converts context into direction and quality |
| Account manager | Moves work, approvals, and client communication |
| Creator | Produces governed outputs from approved inputs |
| Client stakeholder | Reviews, approves, and requests work |
| Engineering and design | Need a precise operating standard to build against |

## 8. Delivery Principle

The product should be built in the sequence:

**source of truth -> governed context -> strategy -> execution -> approval -> collaboration -> selective autonomy**
