This document is the single source of truth for who SMMAHUB is for, how it is positioned, and how it is priced. Every other doc, screen, agent output, onboarding step, and pricing surface must conform to it. Where another doc conflicts, this document wins and the other doc must be corrected.

## 1. The locked ICP — Tier 2 operating agencies

SMMAHUB is built **exclusively** for established social media marketing agencies that are already running a real business.

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Active clients | 5–25 |
| Monthly revenue | €15k–€100k |
| Team | 2–10 people (owner + account managers, editors, strategists) |
| Stage | Operating and delivering, not still searching for product-market fit |
| Buying behavior | Will pay €199–€499/month without friction if the product is genuinely better than their current chaos |
| Decision bar | Already runs a real business and will **instantly** detect anything shallow, fake, or demo-grade |

The core pains of this ICP are operational, not aspirational:

- Context lives in one person's head and gets re-explained constantly.
- Delivery happens across Slack, Google Docs, spreadsheets, DMs, and memory.
- Approvals are chased manually and stall.
- Strategy quality and client-facing professionalism vary by who happens to do the work.
- The owner is the bottleneck on every non-trivial decision.

We win when an account manager spends less time hunting for context, the owner reviews fewer low-leverage decisions, and the agency looks more premium to its clients than its competitors do.

## 2. Who SMMAHUB is explicitly NOT for

We do not build for, market to, price for, or design onboarding around:

- **Tier 1 "aspirational" agencies** — 0–3 clients, under €5k/month, often following a YouTube guru, still figuring it out.
- Solo freelancers and one-person operations with no team and no delivery system.
- Beginners trying to land their first client.
- Free-trial collectors, discount-seekers, and tire-kickers who churn at the first bump.

This is roughly 80% of people who call themselves "SMMA owners." They will not pay, and building for them is a trap that dilutes the product into a shallow content tool. We deliberately leave this market to other tools.

If a feature, screen, onboarding question, or piece of copy only makes sense for someone with 0–3 clients trying to get started, it does not belong in SMMAHUB.

## 3. Positioning — infrastructure, not a tool

SMMAHUB is **operating infrastructure** for an agency — the governed system its delivery runs on. It is not:

- a content generator with agency branding,
- a dashboard with AI buttons,
- a project manager with prompts,
- a cheap subscription tool.

The mental model is closer to the infrastructure an established agency adopts and standardizes on (the way teams adopt Linear, Vercel, or HubSpot) than to a €29 app they try and abandon. Pricing, copy, onboarding, and the quality bar all reflect that. We sell the replacement of operational chaos and owner-bottlenecking, not "AI features."

## 4. Pricing — locked floor €199/month

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Pricing floor | **€199/month** — nothing is sold below this |
| Free plan | **None.** Removed from all product thinking and surfaces |
| Range | €199–€499/month (plus custom for larger agencies) |
| Frame | Infrastructure / operating system, billed accordingly |
| Top of funnel | A booked strategy audit / guided demo with a real operator — not a self-serve free signup that invites churn |

Indicative tier structure (names may be refined, the economics may not drop below the floor):

| Tier | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| Operate | €199/mo | Established agency, up to ~10 active clients |
| Scale | €349/mo | Growing agency, up to ~25 active clients, multi-seat team |
| Agency | €499/mo (or custom) | Larger team, advanced governance, higher client volume |

There is no free tier and no beginner tier. The entry point is a paying, operating agency. A time-boxed trial, if offered at all, is gated (card or qualification) and framed as evaluation by a serious buyer — never as a free playground.

## 5. The quality bar this ICP demands

Because the buyer already runs a real agency, every surface must survive an expert's five-second judgment:

- **Agent output** must be specific, agency-grounded, and immediately usable in real client delivery — never generic AI text an operator would discard.
- **Onboarding** must feel like configuring serious infrastructure, not filling a beginner's intake form. It respects the owner's time and assumes competence.
- **The client portal** must make the agency look more premium than its competitors — it is part of the product moat and is shown to the agency's own paying clients.
- **Depth over breadth.** A few workflows done to operator standard beat many shallow ones.

If an experienced agency owner would look at a screen or an output and think "this is shallow" or "this was built for beginners," it fails the bar and must be rebuilt.

## 6. How to apply this document

- Personas, JTBD, market, and positioning docs describe only the Tier 2 operator. Remove beginner/solo personas.
- Monetization and pricing docs use the €199 floor and the no-free-plan rule.
- Feature prioritization favors operational leverage for a real team over enablement for a beginner.
- Onboarding and UX copy assume an experienced operator with clients and a team.
- Marketing/landing copy sells infrastructure and operational outcomes, priced from €199.
