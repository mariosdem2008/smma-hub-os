# 22 Product Freeze

| Related | [21-launch-acceptance-criteria](21-launch-acceptance-criteria.md), [24-execution-roadmap](24-execution-roadmap.md) |

## 1. Freeze Purpose

Freeze defines the point where launch-critical scope stops expanding and the team shifts to hardening, documentation, validation, and defect resolution.

## 2. Freeze Rules

- no new major workflows
- no new platform-category expansion
- no unvalidated autonomy features
- only defect fixes, polish, launch documentation, and critical safety work

## 3. Exit Conditions

- acceptance criteria are met
- launch docs match product behavior
- pilot feedback is reviewed and acted on
- known critical risks have owners and mitigation paths
