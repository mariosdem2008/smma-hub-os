## 1. North Star

SMMAHUB should feel like one governed operating environment for Tier 2 operating agencies where agency standards, client truth, strategy, delivery work, and AI actions remain connected.

## 2. Core Promise

The promise is simple:

**Turn an agency's expertise into a repeatable operating system.**

That means:

- define agency rules once
- apply them across clients without re-explaining context
- convert strategy into governed execution
- keep approvals and collaboration inside the same system
- let AI work from rules and evidence, not around them

## 3. End-State Model

SMMAHUB becomes the system where:

1. An established agency with clients and a team encodes positioning, offers, standards, and approvals.
2. Each client gets a durable operating record.
3. Governed strategy is generated from the record and active subject packs.
4. Strategy produces briefs, tasks, approvals, and client-visible progress.
5. Specialist agents help across setup, strategy, execution, and reporting.
6. The platform keeps an audit trail for important actions and decisions.

## 4. Product Invariants

- Strategy should outlive a single draft.
- Execution should point back to a strategy, request, or approved decision.
- AI outputs should reference source context and pack rules.
- Risk should determine review depth and approval routing.
- Client collaboration should reflect current work state, not stale snapshots.
- Tenant boundaries should survive every feature and integration.

## 5. What Great Looks Like

- An owner can configure the agency model once and reuse it.
- A strategist can generate a grounded brief instead of starting from a blank page.
- An account manager can explain status without hunting across tools.
- A creator receives an approved brief, not an ambiguous ask.
- A client sees a premium, structured service experience.

## 6. Vision Boundary

SMMAHUB is not trying to replace the agency or teach people how to start one. It is operating infrastructure for agencies that already have clients, team members, and delivery standards, and it removes the repetitive coordination work that prevents expertise from scaling.
