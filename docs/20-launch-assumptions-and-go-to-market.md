## 1. Launch Hypothesis

The first successful customers will be Tier 2 agencies that already have delivery discipline but lack a governed operating system tying setup, strategy, execution, approvals, and client collaboration together.

## 2. Initial Customer Profile

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Active clients | 5-25 |
| Monthly revenue | EUR15k-EUR100k |
| Team | 2-10 people |
| Service model | Social, creative, paid social, or content-led delivery |
| Pain level | High context loss, high approval friction, founder bottlenecks |
| Readiness | Willing to do serious setup in exchange for repeatable leverage |
| Buying behavior | Will pay EUR199-EUR499/month if the product is genuinely better than the current chaos |

## 3. Launch Assumptions

- Agencies will tolerate setup if time-to-value is visible.
- Strategy quality is the strongest credibility wedge.
- Client-facing professionalism increases perceived product value.
- Governance and approval integrity matter more than flashy autonomy.

## 4. GTM Motion

1. Sell the operating-infrastructure story, not isolated AI features.
2. Use booked strategy audits and guided demos as the top-of-funnel motion.
3. Use pilot agencies to validate the full loop.
4. Lead with repeatability, trust, and professionalism.
5. Capture proof through before-and-after workflow improvements.
6. Do not use a self-serve no-cost trial collector loop.

## 5. Launch Risks

- setup burden exceeds visible early value
- product feels broad but not coherent
- AI quality is inconsistent under real client conditions
- portal quality lags internal ambition
