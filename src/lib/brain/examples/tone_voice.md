# Tone & Voice Guidelines

## Voice Adjectives

Our brand voice is:

| Trait | Description | In Practice |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Professional** | Knowledgeable and credible | Use data, cite sources, be accurate |
| **Approachable** | Warm and relatable | Use "you" and "we", share stories |
| **Confident** | Assured without arrogance | State opinions clearly, own expertise |
| **Helpful** | Genuinely useful | Lead with value, answer questions |
| **Concise** | Clear and efficient | Short sentences, no fluff |
| **Witty** | Clever when appropriate | Occasional wordplay, never forced |

### Voice Spectrum

## Banned Words & Phrases

### Corporate Jargon (Never Use)

| Word/Phrase | Why It's Banned |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Synergy | Overused, meaningless |
| Leverage (as verb) | Corporate cliche |
| Circle back | Just say "follow up" |
| Low-hanging fruit | Overused metaphor |
| Move the needle | Vague, cliche |
| Game-changer | Hyperbolic |
| Paradigm shift | Pretentious |
| Deep dive | Use "detailed look" |
| Bandwidth | Use "capacity" or "time" |
| Touch base | Just say "connect" |
| Disrupt/Disruption | Overused startup speak |
| At the end of the day | Filler phrase |

### Filler Phrases (Avoid)

- "In today's fast-paced digital landscape..."
- "It goes without saying..."
- "Needless to say..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- "To be honest..."
- "In my humble opinion..."

## Writing Rules

### The Golden Rules

1. **Use active voice**
   - Bad: "The content was created by our team"
   - Good: "Our team created the content"

2. **Keep sentences under 20 words**
   - Bad: "In order to effectively optimize your social media presence and ensure maximum engagement with your target audience, you should consider..."
   - Good: "Want better engagement? Here's how to optimize your social presence."

3. **One idea per paragraph**
   - Each paragraph should have a single, clear point
   - If you're covering multiple ideas, break them up

4. **Use bullet points for lists**
   - More than 3 items? Use bullets
   - Makes content scannable
   - Reduces cognitive load

5. **Lead with the benefit**
   - Bad: "Our process includes 5 steps that will help you grow"
   - Good: "Grow your audience with our 5-step process"

6. **End with action**
   - Every piece should have a clear next step
   - Don't leave readers wondering "so what?"

### Formatting Guidelines

- Use headers to break up long content
- Bold key phrases for emphasis
- Use numbers for sequential steps
- Use bullets for non-sequential lists
- Keep paragraphs to 3-4 lines max
- Include visual breaks every 150-200 words

## Examples

### Good Examples

**LinkedIn Post:**
> Your content isn't working because it lacks a clear strategy.
>
> Here's the fix:
>
> 1. Define your content pillars (3-4 themes you'll own)
> 2. Create a posting rhythm (consistency > frequency)
> 3. Lead with value (teach, don't preach)
>
> The brands winning on social aren't posting more.
> They're posting with purpose.
>
> What's your biggest content challenge?

**Email Opening:**
> Quick question: When's the last time your content strategy got a refresh?
>
> If you're like most teams I work with, it's been a while.
>
> Here's why that matters...

### Bad Examples

**Avoid This:**
> In today's fast-paced digital landscape, it has become increasingly apparent that content strategies that lack cohesive implementation often fail to deliver the meaningful results that businesses are seeking in their quest for enhanced engagement and brand visibility across multiple social media platforms.

**Why It's Bad:**
- 52 words in one sentence
- Passive voice ("it has become")
- Corporate jargon ("digital landscape")
- No clear takeaway
- No personality
