# Strategy SOP

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Allocation | Purpose | Examples |
|--------|------------|---------|----------|
| **Educational** | 40% | Establish authority, provide value | How-tos, tutorials, industry insights, tips & tricks |
| **Engagement** | 30% | Build community, drive interaction | Polls, questions, debates, trend reactions |
| **Promotional** | 20% | Drive conversions, showcase results | Case studies, testimonials, offers, CTAs |
| **Culture** | 10% | Humanize brand, build connection | Behind-the-scenes, team spotlights, values |

---

## Strategy Process

### Phase 1: Discovery (Week 1)
1. **Intake Call** (60 min)
   - Business goals and KPIs
   - Target audience deep-dive
   - Brand voice preferences
   - Content history review

2. **Questionnaire Completion**
   - Brand guidelines upload
   - Competitor list
   - Content examples they admire
   - Topics to avoid

### Phase 2: Research (Week 1-2)
1. **Competitor Analysis**
   - Content audit of 3-5 competitors
   - Engagement pattern analysis
   - Gap identification

2. **Audience Research**
   - Persona development
   - Pain point mapping
   - Content preference survey

### Phase 3: Strategy Development (Week 2)
1. **Content Pillar Definition**
   - Pillar rationale document
   - Topic clusters per pillar
   - Content format recommendations

2. **Editorial Calendar Creation**
   - Monthly themes
   - Weekly posting schedule
   - Content type rotation

### Phase 4: Execution (Ongoing)
1. **Weekly Content Production**
   - Batch content creation
   - Review and approval cycle
   - Scheduling and publishing

2. **Optimization Loop**
   - Weekly performance check
   - Monthly analytics review
   - Quarterly strategy refresh

## Content Frequency Guidelines

### Weekly Distribution
```
Monday:    Educational post + engagement prompt
Tuesday:   Educational post + story content
Wednesday: Engagement post + promotional teaser
Thursday:  Educational post + engagement post
Friday:    Cultural/behind-scenes + weekly wrap-up
Weekend:   Evergreen repurpose or rest
```

### Content Types Per Week
- 3-4 Educational posts
- 2-3 Engagement posts
- 1-2 Promotional posts
- 1 Culture post
- 5-7 Story/ephemeral content

## Planning Cadence

| Cadence | Activity | Participants |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| **Daily** | Engagement monitoring, trend spotting | Social team |
| **Weekly** | Content batching, performance pulse check | Content + Strategy |
| **Bi-weekly** | Client sync call, content approval | Client + Account lead |
| **Monthly** | Strategy review, analytics deep-dive | Full team + Client |
| **Quarterly** | Strategy refresh, goal realignment | Leadership + Client |

---

## Approval Workflow

1. **Draft Creation** - Content creator
2. **Internal Review** - Peer review for quality
3. **Strategy Check** - Alignment with pillars
4. **Client Preview** - Optional based on package
5. **Final QA** - Grammar, links, hashtags
6. **Schedule** - Queue for posting
7. **Post-Publish** - Monitor and engage
