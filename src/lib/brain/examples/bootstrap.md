- Inconsistent posting schedule
- No clear content strategy
- Founder has no time for thought leadership

**Resource Challenges:**

- Can't hire full-time content person

- Lack of strategic direction
- No time to learn social best practices

**Business Challenges:**
- Need to build credibility in market
- Competitors are more visible
- Sales team needs air cover
- Investors want to see brand presence
- Recruiting requires employer brand
