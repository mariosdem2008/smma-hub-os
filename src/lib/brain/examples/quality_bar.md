# Quality Bar

## Review Criteria

Every piece of content must pass these quality checks before publication:

### The 8-Point Quality Checklist

| # | Criterion | Weight | Description |
|---|-----------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | **Brand Alignment** | 15% | Matches voice, tone, and brand guidelines |
| 2 | **Value Delivery** | 20% | Provides clear, actionable takeaway |
| 3 | **Hook Effectiveness** | 15% | Captures attention in first 3 seconds |
| 4 | **CTA Clarity** | 10% | Clear next step for the audience |
| 5 | **Visual Quality** | 10% | Professional, on-brand design/media |
| 6 | **Platform Optimization** | 10% | Properly formatted for each platform |
| 7 | **Grammar & Spelling** | 10% | Error-free, properly punctuated |
| 8 | **Timeliness** | 10% | Relevant to current events/trends |

### Scoring Guide

| Score | Rating | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 90-100% | Excellent | Approve immediately |
| 80-89% | Good | Approve with minor notes |
| 70-79% | Acceptable | Approve with revisions |
| 60-69% | Needs Work | Revision required |
| Below 60% | Reject | Major rewrite needed |

---

## Acceptance Threshold

### Minimum Requirements

- **Overall Score**: Must achieve 80% or higher
- **Critical Criteria**: Brand Alignment and Value Delivery must both pass
- **No Zeros**: No criterion can score 0/10
- **Minimum 6 of 8**: At least 6 criteria must pass (7/10 or higher)

### Non-Negotiables

These elements must ALWAYS be present:

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Brand voice | Must sound like the client's brand |
| Value | Must teach, inspire, or entertain |
| Error-free | Zero typos or grammatical errors |
| Platform-ready | Correct format, length, hashtags |
| Original | No plagiarism or unattributed content |

## Revision Policy

### Standard Revision Process

| Round | Expectation | Turnaround |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| **Round 1** | Major feedback incorporated | 24 hours |
| **Round 2** | Minor adjustments | 12 hours |
| **Round 3** | Final polish only | 6 hours |

### Revision Limits

- **Standard**: 3 revision rounds per content piece
- **After 3 rounds**: Senior review required
- **Scope creep**: New requests = new content piece
- **Timeline impact**: Revisions may affect publishing schedule

### What Counts as a Revision

**IS a Revision:**
- Feedback on drafted content
- Changes to copy or visuals
- Adjustments to messaging

**NOT a Revision:**
- New direction or concept
- Additional platforms
- Expanded scope

## Escalation Triggers

### Immediate Escalation Required

| Trigger | Escalate To | Action |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| **Client Dissatisfaction** | Account Manager | Same-day response |
| **Brand Safety Issue** | Content Lead + Legal | Pause publication |
| **Compliance Concern** | Operations | Review before posting |
| **Deadline at Risk** | Content Lead | Resource reallocation |
| **Quality Dispute** | Senior Strategist | Final decision |

### Warning Signs to Watch

| Signal | Meaning | Response |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Same feedback twice | Process issue | Review workflow |
| Client frustration | Expectation gap | Schedule sync call |
| Repeated revisions | Unclear brief | Re-do discovery |
| Quality drop | Capacity issue | Resource check |

## Quality Assurance Process

### The 5-Step QA Flow

```
Step 1: Creator Self-Review
        ↓
Step 2: Peer Review
        ↓
Step 3: Client Preview (if applicable)
        ↓
Step 4: Final QA Check
        ↓
Step 5: Schedule & Publish
```

### Step Details

#### Step 1: Creator Self-Review
- [ ] Run through 8-point checklist
- [ ] Read content aloud
- [ ] Check all links and tags
- [ ] Verify platform formatting
- [ ] Score self-assessment

#### Step 2: Peer Review
- [ ] Fresh eyes review
- [ ] Check against brand guidelines
- [ ] Verify factual accuracy
- [ ] Assess engagement potential
- [ ] Provide constructive feedback

#### Step 3: Client Preview
*When applicable based on package*
- [ ] Share via approval system
- [ ] Allow 24-48hr review window
- [ ] Incorporate feedback
- [ ] Confirm approval received

#### Step 4: Final QA Check
- [ ] All revisions incorporated
- [ ] Final proofread
- [ ] Platform-specific check
- [ ] Scheduling verified
- [ ] Assets confirmed

#### Step 5: Post-Publish Audit
- [ ] Verify successful publication
- [ ] Check for immediate issues
- [ ] Monitor initial engagement
- [ ] Document any learnings

## Quality Metrics

### Content Quality KPIs

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| First-pass approval rate | >80% | % approved without revision |
| Average revision rounds | <1.5 | Mean revisions per piece |
| Client satisfaction score | >4.5/5 | Monthly feedback survey |
| Error rate | <1% | Errors per 100 posts |
| On-time delivery | >95% | Posts delivered by deadline |

| Monthly | Quality metrics review |
| Quarterly | Process optimization |

## Common Quality Issues & Fixes

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Weak hooks | Template fatigue | Refresh hook library |
| Off-brand voice | New creator | Deeper onboarding |
| Missing CTAs | Oversight | Checklist enforcement |
| Formatting errors | Rush | Buffer time in schedule |
| Typos | No proofreading | Mandatory peer review |
| Generic content | Poor brief | Better discovery process |
