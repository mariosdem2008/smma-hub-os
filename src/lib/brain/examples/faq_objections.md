**Answer:**

**Explore:**
> "Can I ask what went wrong? Understanding that helps me know if we're the right fit."

**Respond (based on common issues):**

| Their Issue | Our Difference |
|-------------|----------------|
| "Content felt generic" | We have a deep onboarding process and dedicated strategist who learns your voice |
| "No results" | We focus on strategy first, set realistic KPIs, and report transparently |
| "Poor communication" | You'll have a dedicated point of contact and regular check-ins |

> "Actually, specialized industries often perform better on social because the content stands out. Generic advice is everywhere - specific expertise is rare."

**Evidence:**
> "We've worked with [similar industry] and can share anonymized case studies. Our onboarding process is designed to capture your unique expertise and translate it into engaging content."

> "We don't expect to be experts in your field - you are. We're experts at turning your knowledge into content that resonates. That's the partnership."
