## Format Guidelines

### LinkedIn Posts
- **Length**: 1200-1500 characters optimal
- **Structure**: Hook > Story/Value > CTA
- **Formatting**:
  - One sentence per line
  - Use line breaks liberally
  - Emojis sparingly (1-2 max)
  - No hashtags in body, 3-5 in comments

### Instagram Captions
- **Above fold**: 150-300 characters (hook + key message)
- **Full caption**: 500-1000 characters
- **Structure**: Hook > Value > CTA > Hashtags
- **Hashtags**: 5-15 relevant tags

### Twitter/X Threads
- **Thread length**: 5-8 tweets optimal
- **Each tweet**: Should stand alone
- **Structure**: Hook tweet > Value tweets > CTA tweet
- **Engagement**: Add self-reply for conversation

### Video Scripts
- **Short-form (Reels/TikTok)**: 30-60 seconds
- **Structure**: Hook (3 sec) > Content (20-50 sec) > CTA (5 sec)
- **Pacing**: Fast cuts, clear audio, captions

## Quality Checklist

### Pre-Publication Review

- [ ] **Hook test**: Would I stop scrolling for this?
- [ ] **Value check**: Is there a clear takeaway?
- [ ] **CTA present**: Is there a next step?
- [ ] **Voice match**: Does this sound like the brand?
- [ ] **Error-free**: Spelling, grammar, links work?
- [ ] **Platform optimized**: Right format and length?
- [ ] **Visual quality**: Images/video are high quality?
- [ ] **Accessibility**: Captions, alt text included?

### The 3-Second Test
> If someone saw this for 3 seconds, would they:
> 1. Know what it's about?
> 2. Want to keep reading/watching?
> 3. Know who it's from?

## Content Formulas

### The AIDA Formula
1. **Attention**: Hook that stops the scroll
2. **Interest**: Build curiosity with story/stat
3. **Desire**: Show the transformation/benefit
4. **Action**: Clear CTA

### The PAS Formula
1. **Problem**: Identify the pain point
2. **Agitate**: Make them feel it
3. **Solution**: Present your answer

### The BAB Formula
1. **Before**: Current state/problem
2. **After**: Desired state/outcome
3. **Bridge**: How to get there
